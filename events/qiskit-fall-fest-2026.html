Overview

QCB | IBM Quantum | BGA

How do researchers build confidence in their results as quantum computations become difficult to verify classically? We hosted a panel and hackathon themed around what goes on behind and beyond Qiskit. We worked with hardware limitations, mitigated errors, and assessed whether computational results were trustworthy and useful. The panel discussion connected the technical decisions behind quantum experiments with broader questions about applications, quantum advantage, and the skills students need to contribute to the quantum computing industry.

Featured Speakers

Dr. Brad Mitchell

Staff Research Scientist, IBM Quantum

Dr. Soyoung Shin (Sophy)

Quantum Developer Advocate, IBM Quantum

Dr. Brad Mitchell is a Staff Research Scientist at IBM Quantum, where he focuses on demonstrating and extending the capabilities of the IBM's Quantum Computers, using methods like error mitigation. Brad completed his PhD at UC Berkeley in Prof. Irfan Siddiqi's group, where he implemented novel entangling interactions between superconducting qubits.

Dr. Soyoung Shin (Sophy) is a Quantum Developer Advocate at IBM Quantum who develops educational content and hands-on learning experiences for students and researchers. In addition to her work in quantum computing, she serves as an APS Career Mentoring Fellow, helping students and early-career professionals navigate their career paths in science and technology.
