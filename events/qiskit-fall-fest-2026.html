Overview

QCB | IBM Quantum | BGA

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 is an annual student-led quantum computing event organized by QCB and BGA in partnership with IBM Quantum. The event brings together students, researchers, and quantum enthusiasts across UC Berkeley and the Bay Area to build, learn, and explore quantum information science.

We are excited to announce that the Santa Clara team of IBM will participate directly in our Fall Fest. IBM engineers and researchers will deliver a featured panel talk, host an interactive social mixer, and provide an afternoon hackathon challenge track with dedicated mentorship.
